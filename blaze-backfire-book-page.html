Blaze Backfire

Love Only #8

Coming January 11 · Available now for pre-order

Pre-order on AmazonRead the Blurb

One shared wall. One router on the wrong side of it. One firefighter determined to be the quietest neighbor in Maine.

Ashley Langerfeld manages the lives of the very rich from the front seat of her Honda Civic, armed with twenty feet of extension cord and three bars of borrowed WiFi. She could solve her problems by asking her handsome neighbor for help, but Ashley doesn’t ask anyone for anything. Especially not a man who makes her forget what she was doing.

Tyler Lowrey is a firefighter who feeds half the town, fixes everything in sight, and refuses to let anyone buy him so much as a sundae. He’s perfectly capable of handling a five-alarm emergency. The fiercely independent woman next door? That’s another story.

Between meddling townspeople, a two-person kayak, and a firefighter whose good intentions sometimes go a little too far, Ashley is discovering that falling in love is the one thing she can’t organize.

And Tyler is about to learn that not every problem can be solved by rescuing someone.

A sweet, clean small-town romantic comedy from the Love Only series. Book 8. Releasing January 11 — available now for pre-order.
