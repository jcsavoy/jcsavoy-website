Return to Whispering Falls with this delightful collection of sweet romances.

Filled with holiday charm, heartwarming connections, and second chances just waiting to bloom.

This boxed set includes books 4–6 in the beloved Whispering Falls Sweet Romance series—perfect for readers who adore emotional depth, charming townsfolk, and happily-ever-afters set against the backdrop of love and laughter.

Briefs and Bouquets: Ambitious Chicago lawyer Willow Davies only plans to visit Whispering Falls long enough to help with her sister’s wedding. But a surprise pairing with laid-back best man Rowan McCarthy—and an unexpected hurricane—leave her rethinking everything. Trapped together in a storm shelter, sparks fly and priorities shift. Is it possible that the life she’s always planned isn’t the one her heart truly wants?

Decorating with Love: Interior designer Joy Fletcher is determined to save her struggling business with one big job—decorating the home of handsome new town doctor, Cameron Browning. When his ex returns hoping to rekindle their past, Cameron invents a fake engagement with Joy. What starts as a harmless ruse turns into something deeper. But can love built on a lie survive when the truth comes out?

Mistletoe and Heartstrings: Bestselling author Donovan Butler needs a break from the spotlight—and his heartbreak. Whispering Falls seems like the perfect escape—until he meets Tina Wilson, the lively café owner with a mystery on her hands and a secret admirer of her own. As Christmas magic swirls around them, letters are exchanged, hearts begin to heal, and a winter retreat may become something much more permanent.

From festive holidays to stolen kisses and unexpected twists, these heartfelt love stories will warm your heart and leave you wishing Whispering Falls was your hometown too.
