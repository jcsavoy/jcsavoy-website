Return to Whispering Falls for three unforgettable sweet romances.

Unexpected connections, hidden secrets, and second chances prove that love has a way of rewriting even the best-laid plans.

This includes books 10–12 in the beloved Whispering Falls Sweet Romance series—perfect for readers who adore charming small towns, irresistible chemistry, a touch of mystery, and heartwarming happily-ever-afters.

Code Red for Love: Tech genius Luke Hopkins has no time for romance, especially when ambitious entrepreneur Paige Miller arrives at a Boston conference with a matchmaking app that rivals his own. But when mysterious glitches threaten both their businesses, the competitors must join forces to uncover the culprit. Between late-night coding sessions, witty banter, and unexpected sparks, these rivals may discover that the perfect match has nothing to do with algorithms.

Hearts in Hiding: Bestselling novelist Sage Collins can’t resist a good mystery, especially when former investigative journalist Gabriel Cross moves into Whispering Falls’ infamous Morgan estate. The crumbling Victorian holds a dark history of murder, secrets, and rumored hidden treasures. When a forgotten diary draws them into the mansion’s mysterious past, Sage and Gabriel discover that uncovering old secrets might be easier than admitting what’s happening to their own hearts.

Blueprint of the Heart: Boston architect Charlie Reynolds returns to Whispering Falls determined to restore a neglected Victorian into a pottery studio and rebuild her career. The last thing she needs is a stubborn local contractor questioning her plans. But Rider Summers has ideas of his own, and their clashes over construction details soon give way to unexpected attraction. Between snowstorm strolls, kitchen mishaps, and small-town charm, Charlie begins to wonder if the future she’s been designing has been missing its most important feature—love.

From rival matchmaking apps and mysterious Victorian mansions to renovations that mend more than old walls, these three delightful romances prove that the best discoveries aren’t always the ones we’re looking for. Come home to Whispering Falls, where every new beginning holds the promise of happily ever after.
