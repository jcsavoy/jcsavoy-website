Hold Still, Cowboy

The Kingsleys #9

Coming December 14 · Available now for pre-order

Pre-order on Amazon Read the Blurb

He counts everything. She reads what the numbers can’t.

Shane Kingsley came home to Cottonwood Ridge to run the numbers on his father’s biggest horse sale of the year—a job he’s been doing instead of building something of his own. A former Austin tech founder who lost it all, he trusts data over instinct and keeps his cards close to his chest.

Then the sale horses start turning up hurt in ways that shouldn’t be happening, and the one person who can read what’s really wrong is a traveling equine massage therapist who reads a body like a ledger he never authorized.

She works on instinct. He works on spreadsheets. And the harder Shane tries to solve her like a problem, the more he realizes the thing he wants most is the one thing he can’t measure.

Hold Still, Cowboy is the story of a man learning to want something he can’t quantify—and to act, for once, without a backup plan.

A sweet, small-town Christmas cowboy romance and Book 9 in The Kingsleys of Cottonwood Ridge series. Releasing December 14 — available now for pre-order.
