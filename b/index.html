Permanent book links

Use these in the back matter of your books. They never change, even if the website is rebuilt or moved — each one forwards to that book’s current page on jcsavoy.com.

These are not affiliate links, so they are safe for the back of a book. The reader lands on your book page and clicks Buy on Amazon from there.

The only thing that must stay the same for these to keep working is the domain jcsavoy.com and the /b/ folder.
