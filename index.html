Meet JC Savoy

I grew up in a charming small town in New England, where the quaint streets and friendly neighbors left an indelible mark on my heart — and I’ve been chasing that feeling on the page ever since.

After many years as an educator, I traded lesson plans for love stories. Now I spend my days creating the kind of romances I’ve always loved to read — clean, sweet, and full of heart.

My stories take you to places where everyday folk smile and say hello, cowboys tip their hats, billionaires have hearts of gold, and small towns hold the biggest secrets.

Each night, you’ll find me curled up with a Hallmark movie — a swoon-worthy romance or a delightful mystery. That nightly ritual fuels my passion for these stories.

I believe romance should make you smile, sigh, and stay up way past your bedtime turning pages. No cliffhangers on the happily ever after — that’s guaranteed.

— JC
