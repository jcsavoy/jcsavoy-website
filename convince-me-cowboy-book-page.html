Convince Me, Cowboy

The Kingsleys #8

Buy on Amazon Read the Blurb

One fake date. One very real problem.

Harper Kingsley doesn't need a date to the Cottonwood Ridge Gala. She especially doesn't need one hand-picked by her meddling father and delivered to her feed store in boots and flannel, buying mineral blocks he clearly doesn't need.

Cameron Humphries left a high-powered Denver career to build a life on his grandmother's Montana ranch. He agreed to one evening as a favor to his father. One dance. One dinner. No complications.

That was the plan.

But Cameron wasn't supposed to memorize the way she laughed before she could stop it. Harper wasn't supposed to notice that he listened as if every word she said mattered. And neither of them was supposed to let the whole town believe they were together—except neither corrected it, and the silence started telling a truth they weren't ready to say out loud.

Harper is an expert at taking care of everyone else and keeping her own heart under lock and key. Cameron is a man who falls fast, falls hard, and has no idea how to stand still long enough to let someone love him back.

One of them is going to break first. The only question is whether the other one will still be standing there when it happens.

Convince Me, Cowboy is a sweet, slow-burn fake-date romance and Book 8 in The Kingsleys of Cottonwood Ridge series.
