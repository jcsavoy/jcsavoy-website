The Kingsleys #11

Pre-order on AmazonRead the Blurb

He came back for the land. He didn’t count on her.

Last winter, Carver Lockhart kissed Delaney Kingsley like he meant forever. Then he shook her hand, drove away, and broke her heart.

Now he’s back in Cottonwood Ridge, determined to buy a piece of her family’s ranch. And apparently determined to pretend those kisses never happened.

Delaney has no intention of falling for the handsome, infuriating carpenter twice. But when a damaged barn threatens the biggest event of her career, Carver is the one man who can save it.

Working side by side brings back feelings neither can deny. But Carver is keeping a secret that could tear them apart, and Delaney isn’t about to give her heart to a man who won’t trust her with the truth.

This time, if Carver wants a second chance, he’ll have to earn it.

A sweet, small-town cowboy romance about second chances, family secrets, and a love worth fighting for. Book 11 in The Kingsleys of Cottonwood Ridge series. Releasing February 15, 2027 — available now for pre-order.
